# Portfolio

Nicolas Saavedra's personal site at nicolassaavedra.com: a public record of his professional work, written for the people who judge it on substance.

## Language

### Readers

**Peer**:
The primary reader: a founder, engineer or potential collaborator assessing Nicolas's work and how he thinks. When reader needs conflict, the Peer wins.
_Avoid_: Visitor, user, audience

**Recruiter**:
The secondary reader: someone evaluating Nicolas for a role, who needs a scannable work history and a way to reach him.
_Avoid_: HR, hiring manager, talent

**Open to Work**:
The site state in which Recruiter needs (the CV and the Timeline) are promoted ahead of Peer content. Off by default.
_Avoid_: Hiring mode, job-hunting mode

### Content

**Case Study**:
An in-depth account of one piece of work, told as the problem, what Nicolas did, and the outcome. The site's showcase for Peers.
_Avoid_: Project, portfolio item, showcase

**Selected Work**:
The curated set of Case Studies on the site.
_Avoid_: Projects, portfolio

**Timeline**:
The chronological, scannable record of Nicolas's roles and education, split into Tracks. The site's showcase for Recruiters.
_Avoid_: Experience, résumé, work history

**Track**:
One strand of the Timeline: Engineering (building production software) or Teaching (instructing and tutoring). Education sits beside the Tracks, not in one.
_Avoid_: Category, section

**Pillar**:
One of the three areas Nicolas claims as his focus (infrastructure & AWS, observability, building and reviewing product code), stated in the intro rather than as a skills list.
_Avoid_: Skill, specialty, expertise

**Signature Piece**:
The single interactive, animated element on the site, drawn from Nicolas's own work. Everything else stays content-first.
_Avoid_: Hero animation, widget, demo

**Request Automaton**:
The Signature Piece: the reader's own page load, from lookup to delivery by the server running the site, drawn as a state machine.
_Avoid_: Request diagram, infra graph, flowchart

**CV**:
The downloadable document generated from the site's content. Never edited by hand.
_Avoid_: Résumé, profile PDF
